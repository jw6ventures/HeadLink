OpenAutoAndroid is an independent, open-source app that drives a car's Android Auto head unit from your phone, without Google's Android Auto app. It is not made by or affiliated with Google or any car maker.

<b>What it does</b>
<ul>
<li>Projects over USB, or wirelessly over the head unit's own Wi-Fi, started from the phone, automatically when the car connects, or from the car's Android Auto button.</li>
<li>Its own offline map and turn-by-turn navigation (Mapsforge maps, BRouter routing), with turn instructions on the instrument cluster where the car supports it.</li>
<li>Media controls and now-playing for whatever is playing on the phone; phone audio stays on Bluetooth.</li>
<li>Destinations shared from OsmAnd or any app, address search from OsmAnd map files, saved places.</li>
<li>Optional live traffic with your own HERE key, and weather from Breezy Weather. Both are off by default.</li>
</ul>

<b>Before you install</b>
Head units only talk to a phone that presents a certificate they trust. OpenAutoAndroid does not ship one: you import a source identity you have obtained yourself (the project's documentation describes how). Without it the app cannot connect.

Tested on a 2024 Ford SYNC 4.0 head unit. Other head units may not work. No certification or vehicle compatibility is claimed. Do not operate the phone while driving.

No ads, no analytics, no account. The app connects to the internet only if you turn on live traffic.
