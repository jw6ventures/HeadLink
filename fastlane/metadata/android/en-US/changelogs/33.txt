OsmAnd maps are now the only maps, for the map, routes, directions and search. Mapsforge files, BRouter files and the drop folder are gone; old files are cleaned up. The OsmAnd maps card is at the top of the Maps tab. Allow tracks and Go around towns work again. A destination outside your maps, or one only a track reaches, says so, and a route between maps that don't touch says a map may be missing. New routes no longer show an exit sign when you stay on the motorway.
