OsmAnd maps are now the only maps: the map, routes, directions and address search all come from the maps you download in OsmAnd. Mapsforge map files, BRouter routing files and the drop folder are gone, and the app cleans up old imported files when it starts. The OsmAnd maps card is now at the top of the Maps tab. A destination outside your maps now says so instead of failing. Newly computed routes no longer show an exit sign when you stay on the motorway.
